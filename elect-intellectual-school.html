Welcome address

Elect Intellectual School exists to help every child grow in knowledge, character and confidence.

We believe a good education is more than passing exams. It is learning to think clearly, work hard and treat other people well.

Our teachers know every pupil by name, and we work closely with parents so that what children learn at school is supported at home.
